    What is a "protection gap"?

    It's the difference between what your family would need if your income stopped, and what they would already have.

    📌 Illustrative example family in Malaysia. Not your numbers.
    Family needsRM 1,120,000
    10 years of living costs (RM 4,500 a month) Home loan left (RM 380k) 2 kids' education (RM 200k)
    Already hasRM 220,000
    Savings & EPF (RM 120k) Group cover from work (RM 100k)
    Protection gap in this example: about RM 900,000. Every family's gap is different, and it changes as life changes.

    🏥 Hospital bills add up fast too. As an illustration, surgery and a few nights in a private hospital can cost tens of thousands of ringgit, and long treatments such as for cancer can cost much more.

    5 easy next steps

    1. Write down what your family spends each month.
    2. List your loans: house, car, credit cards, study loans.
    3. Think about your children's future school and university costs.
    4. Check the cover you already have, including any from your employer.
    5. Review your cover whenever life changes: marriage, a new baby or a new home.
    💬

    Curious about your own family's numbers? Have a friendly chat with your insurance agent. They can help you work out your real protection gap.
